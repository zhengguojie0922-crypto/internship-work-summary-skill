# Resume Writing

Populate the `Resume Wording` section of the final document with three variants for each major output:

1. Conservative: personal action, visible technical scope, and evidence-backed result only.
2. Standard: the same action plus a verified technical effect or validation result.
3. Impactful: use only when evidence supports scope, difficulty, ownership boundary, and outcome.

Match the requested language: Chinese for Chinese requests, English for English requests, and paired Chinese-English wording for bilingual requests. Adapt terminology to the target role; do not mechanically translate or change facts.

Each bullet must name a supported action and may include a runtime or outcome clause only when that exact clause has evidence. Never invent metrics, scale, users, latency, revenue, reliability, novelty, sole ownership, business causality, or production use. Put absent metrics and stronger unverified claims in `Metrics Needs User Input`, not in a resume variant.

Before finalizing, check each variant against its evidence location, attribution boundary, role fit, language, sensitive details, and claim strength. Remove or soften any clause that exceeds the evidence.
